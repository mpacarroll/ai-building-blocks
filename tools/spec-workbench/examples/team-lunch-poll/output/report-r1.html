# Lunch poll idea

Revision 1. Generated by spec-bench from spec.json; edit the workspace, not this file.

## Problem

Every Friday our team wastes twenty minutes in chat arguing about where to get lunch.

## Users

- U1: It's for small office teams, usually 5 to 15 people.

## Goals

- G1: The goal is to pick a place in under five minutes so people stop arguing.

## Non-goals

- N1: No mobile app in v1.
- N2: Integrations with ordering services are out of scope.

## Constraints

- C1: Budget is one developer for two weeks.

## Requirements

1. **R1** (must) Anyone on the team can start a poll with a few restaurant options.
2. **R2** (should) People should be able to vote fast, ideally from their phone.
3. **R3** (must) Votes need to be anonymous.
4. **R4** (must) The organizer must see who voted for what so they can place the order.
5. **R5** (must) The poll must close automatically within 10 minutes.
6. **R6** (must) Polls must stay open at least 15 minutes so remote people can vote.
7. **R7** (must) Voters can change their vote.
8. **R8** (must) Voters cannot change their vote once cast.
9. **R9** (should) Results should show within 2 seconds of the poll closing.
10. **R10** (must) Results must load in under 1 second.
11. **R11** (could) It would be nice to remember everyone's favorite places.
12. **R12** (must) We need to send a reminder before the poll closes. Depends on D1.
13. **R13** (must) Members must get a push notification on the mobile app when a poll opens.

## Decisions

- D1 (open): Slack or email? Options: Slack / email.
- D2 (open): Should ties be broken randomly or by the organizer? Options: randomly / by the organizer.

## Assumptions

- A1: I assume everyone already has a work email address. Validation: none yet.
- A2: Probably most people will vote from a laptop anyway. Validation: none yet.

## Unclassified intake (review these)

- X1 (line 26): Make it look nice.

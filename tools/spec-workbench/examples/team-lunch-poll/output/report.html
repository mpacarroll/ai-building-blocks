# Lunch poll idea

Revision 4. Generated by spec-bench from spec.json; edit the workspace, not this file.

## Problem

Every Friday our team wastes twenty minutes in chat arguing about where to get lunch.

## Users

- U1: It's for small office teams, usually 5 to 15 people.

## Goals

- G1: The goal is to pick a place in under five minutes so people stop arguing.

## Non-goals

- N1: No mobile app in v1.
- N2: Integrations with ordering services are out of scope.

## Constraints

- C1: Budget is one developer for two weeks.
- C2: Use the default component styles of the chosen web framework; no custom visual design in v1.

## Requirements

1. **R1** (must) Any team member can start a poll with 2 to 8 restaurant options.
   - AC1 (draft): Given a signed-in team member on the new poll page, when they enter 1 option, then 9 options, then 3 options and press Start, then 1 and 9 options are rejected with the message "Add 2 to 8 places" and 3 options create an open poll
2. **R2** (must) A voter must be able to cast a vote in at most 2 taps from the poll link, in a phone or laptop browser.
   - AC2 (draft): Given an open poll and its link on a phone browser, when a voter opens the link and taps one place, then the vote is saved after the second tap and the page shows "Vote saved"
3. **R3** (must) A voter must not see how any other person voted. Depends on D3.
   - AC3 (draft): Given an open poll with votes from 3 people, when a voter who is not the organizer views the poll, then the page shows vote counts per place and no voter names
4. **R4** (must) The organizer must see who voted for what so they can place the order. Depends on D3.
   - AC4 (draft): Given a closed poll with votes from 3 people, when the organizer opens the results, then each place lists the names of the people who voted for it
5. **R5** (must) The poll must close automatically 10 minutes after it opens, unless the organizer closes it sooner.
   - AC5 (draft): Given a poll opened at 12:00:00, when no one closes it, then its status is closed at 12:10:00 and new votes are rejected
6. **R7** (must) A voter can change their vote until the poll closes.
   - AC6 (draft): Given a voter who voted for place A in an open poll, when they vote for place B, then the poll counts 1 vote for B and 0 votes for A from that voter
7. **R9** (must) Results must appear within 2 seconds of the poll closing.
   - AC7 (draft): Given a poll with 15 votes, when the poll closes, then the winning place and its vote count are shown to every voter within 2 seconds of the close time
8. **R11** (could) It would be nice to remember everyone's favorite places.
9. **R12** (must) The tool must email every team member a reminder 2 minutes before the poll closes. Depends on D1.
   - AC8 (draft): Given a poll that closes at 12:10:00 for a team of 5, when the clock reaches 12:08:00, then 5 reminder emails are sent, one to each team member
10. **R13** (must) Every team member must get an email with the poll link when a poll opens.
   - AC9 (draft): Given a team of 5, when a member starts a poll, then 5 emails containing the poll link are sent
11. **R14** (must) When two or more places tie, the organizer must pick the winner from the tied places. Depends on D2.
   - AC10 (draft): Given a closed poll where places A and B each have 3 votes, when the organizer opens the results, then the organizer is asked to pick A or B, and the other places are not offered

## Decisions

- D1 (decided): Slack or email? Choice: email. Rationale: A1 says everyone has a work email; not every team uses Slack.
- D2 (decided): Should ties be broken randomly or by the organizer? Choice: the organizer picks from the tied places. Rationale: The organizer places the order anyway, so they break the tie.
- D3 (decided): R3 says votes are anonymous but R4 says the organizer sees who voted for what. Which wins? Choice: hidden from voters, visible to the organizer. Rationale: Found in human review, not by a rule. The organizer needs names to place the order.

## Assumptions

- A1: I assume everyone already has a work email address. Validation: Ask the pilot team's admin before the build starts.
- A2: Probably most people will vote from a laptop anyway. Validation: Count phone versus laptop votes in the first week of pilot polls.

# Lunch poll idea

Every Friday our team wastes twenty minutes in chat arguing about where to get lunch.
The goal is to pick a place in under five minutes so people stop arguing.
It's for small office teams, usually 5 to 15 people.

Anyone on the team can start a poll with a few restaurant options.
People should be able to vote fast, ideally from their phone.
Votes need to be anonymous.
The organizer must see who voted for what so they can place the order.
The poll must close automatically within 10 minutes.
Polls must stay open at least 15 minutes so remote people can vote.
Voters can change their vote.
Voters cannot change their vote once cast.
Results should show within 2 seconds of the poll closing.
Results must load in under 1 second.
It would be nice to remember everyone's favorite places.
We need to send a reminder before the poll closes, Slack or email?
I assume everyone already has a work email address.
Probably most people will vote from a laptop anyway.
No mobile app in v1.
Members must get a push notification on the mobile app when a poll opens.
Should ties be broken randomly or by the organizer?
Integrations with ordering services are out of scope.
Budget is one developer for two weeks.
Make it look nice.
